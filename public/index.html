Photograph & signature

Photo requirements

Photos that do not follow the LCD memorandum guidelines are rejected and you will be asked to send another one. Please check every point below.

Very important: you must be wearing a collared shirt in your photograph.
Picture quality
  • High resolution — blurry, grainy, pixelated or wrong-paper prints are rejected.
  • A clear scan in JPEG format (PNG also works here).
  • Size 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) or passport size (35 × 45 mm).
  • Not too bright, not too dark, no discoloration on the face.
  • No dirt, marks, colouring or seals on the picture.
  • Background: plain solid WHITE, no shadows.
Pose & expression
  • Face the camera directly, full face visible. No tilting or turning of the head — the middle of the mouth and the bridge of the nose should line up on a vertical line through the centre of the picture.
  • Neutral expression, both eyes open, mouth closed. No hair over the eyes; no raised eyebrows, squinting or frowning.
  • A smile with closed lips is acceptable. Do not show teeth or gums.
Clothing, ears, accessories, eyewear
  • Both ears visible where possible; hair neatly arranged or tied back for a full frontal face.
  • Remove earrings, necklaces and hair accessories.
  • Remove eyeglasses.
  • A collared shirt is REQUIRED. Photos in a T-shirt, round-neck, sleeveless or collarless top are rejected.
Signature specimen
  • A clear scanned copy of your signature on a plain white background.
  • Any ink colour is fine, as long as it is clear and readable. If you draw it on screen, use the pad below.

Acceptable samples

Plain white background, head straight, eyes open, mouth closed, ears visible, no accessories.

Will be rejected
  • Selfies, filters, coloured or patterned backgrounds
  • Dark or overexposed pictures, shadows on the face or wall
  • No collared shirt (T-shirts, round necks, sleeveless or collarless tops)
  • Glasses, earrings, necklaces or hair covering the eyes
  • Tilted head, side view, open mouth or teeth showing
  • Photos of a printed photo, stamped or marked pictures
  • Signatures on ruled, coloured or shadowed paper, or too faint to read
